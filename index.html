      About

      I'm Abdulhamid Hassen — a video editor and graphics designer with 5 years of hands-on experience, based in Harar, Ethiopia. I currently work with Harari Broadcasting Network, where I cut and design for broadcast, and I take on freelance projects for YouTubers, brands and agencies alongside that.

      My approach is simple: find the strongest moment, get there faster, and remove anything that gives the viewer a reason to leave. That means aggressive pacing passes, clean audio, and graphics that support the story instead of decorating it.
